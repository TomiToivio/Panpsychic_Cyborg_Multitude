# CYBORG = HUMAN + LLM + LANGUAGE + INTERNET

**Status: operational research proposal (issue #91), not a validated result.**
The formula specifies an assemblage to investigate, not all forms of cognition or a definition
of consciousness. Haraway supplies critical questions about situated hybridity and power;
extended/distributed cognition supplies arguments and methods for testing functional coupling.

## Components and agency boundaries [operational definitions]

| Component | Contribution to investigate | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HUMAN | Embodied experience, goals, judgment, intention and accountability | Named human responsibility for decisions; preserve dissent and the ability to refuse assistance |
| LLM | Linguistic transformation, pattern synthesis, generation and retrieval-mediated reasoning support | Outputs are fallible proposals, with version-dependent biases; no inference to consciousness or autonomous political authority |
| LANGUAGE | Shared signs, external memory, articulation and coordination | Not just a token stream; measure interpretation and uptake; do not claim language is necessary for all cognition |
| INTERNET | Archives, sources, communication, institutions, platforms and feedback | Training exposure is not live access to the entire internet; record accessible sources, retrieval and platform control |

**[Philosophical]** A coupled system may perform cognitive work without being a single subject
of experience. Luhmannian psychic systems are not software objects. A composite actor in PCM
is a provenance and governance model, not evidence of collective consciousness. All mutations
continue through existing service/permission boundaries; capability does not imply authority.

## Comparative protocol [proposed empirical]

Recruit consenting participants with measured baseline skill. Randomize or counterbalance
matched tasks across conditions; hold task instructions, time budget and scoring constant.
Use parallel task versions to limit practice effects, record assistance time, model/version,
settings, prompts and source snapshots, and blind evaluators to condition. Determine sample
size and minimum meaningful effects in advance; report uncertainty and null/negative results.

| Comparison | Task | Hypothesis and outcome | What counts against it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human alone; human + LLM; human + LLM + network sources; human + network sources | Solve a source-verifiable policy question within equal time | H1: assistance improves rubric-scored factual accuracy and calibrated confidence; network-only arm isolates source-access effects | No prespecified meaningful gain, or gains explained by time/source access alone |
| Matched groups, LLM assistance on/off × network access on/off | Make a joint decision from distributed evidence | H2: assistance improves evidence integration and coordination quality while preserving deliberative diversity | More consensus but poorer evidence coverage, minority representation or decision quality |
| Shared glossary/argument map versus ordinary discussion, crossed with assistance | Articulate a common demand from conflicting AI-policy positions | H3: mediated shared language improves mutual understanding and coordination | Vocabulary convergence without improved understanding, or greater exclusion of dissent |
| Assisted tasks followed by unaided transfer after a preregistered delay | Explain and solve a new related problem | H4: complementary roles improve retained learning and transfer | Immediate speed gain accompanied by lower delayed performance or dependency |

**[Design limitation]** The language comparison tests a particular mediation intervention, not
the removal of language from human cognition. Network ablation tests current access, not the
removal of cultural knowledge in training. Do not interpret either as proving a constitutive
claim about all minds. Functional extension needs evidence of reliable, recurrent integration
and complementary roles, not merely a performance increase from an occasional lookup.

## Metrics and failure modes [proposed empirical]

- Accuracy: independently verified claims and citation validity; novelty: blind expert ratings
  alongside duplication checks, never model self-scoring alone.
- Learning: delayed unaided retention and transfer; calibration: confidence versus correctness.
- Coordination: time to a justified decision, integration of distributed evidence, and correction
  of errors; diversity: distinct arguments, participation balance and preserved minority reports.
- Human agency: ability to override recommendations, explain choices and retain effective control;
  triangulate self-reports with observed interventions and task performance.
- Hallucinations: false claims and invented sources. Homogenization and echo chambers: loss of
  independent arguments and cross-group exposure. Deskilling and dependency: degraded unaided
  performance. Power concentration, surveillance and unequal access: ownership/control mapping,
  consent and data-flow audit, and stratified outcomes by access and baseline skill.

Protect participant data; publish consented, minimized evidence with provenance. No consciousness
metric grants permissions. A measured benefit in one population or model does not generalize
without replication; persistent gains must be checked longitudinally.

## Return to the Multitude [interpretive worked example]

An AI-policy group uses an LLM, shared terms and linked sources to discuss “democratic AI.”
Laclau asks how this signifier links demands and draws an antagonistic boundary. Hardt/Negri
ask who produces and governs the common knowledge and infrastructure. Žižek asks how fantasies
of mastery or catastrophe sustain attachment. Peirce helps trace how a slogan's interpretations
change; Luhmann directs attention to which communications generate further communication.
Haraway asks whose situated perspective is excluded and who controls the platform.

Code the conversations, compare network structures and examine human accounts of meaning.
These observations may support or challenge an interpretation of collective agency. They do
not establish a collective mind. PCM may use LaclauGPT findings within this analysis; speculative
premises must not be silently transferred into LaclauGPT or its locked Phase 1 methods.

PHILOSOPHICAL / SPECULATIVE

Panpsychic.
Not a required creed.

PCM takes experience seriously through an exploratory, Spinoza-inspired dual-aspect orientation. Mind and matter may be perspectives on one reality; this analogy does not establish a quantum theory or erase disagreements between ontologies.

Preferred hypothesis

Federico Faggin’s Quantum Information Panpsychism is the preferred speculative route. Preference is not empirical confirmation.

Live alternatives

Kastrup’s idealism, Hoffman’s conscious realism, physicalism, neutral monism and emergentism remain open to comparison and criticism.

Unresolved questions

Combination, shared physical regularities and the relation of mechanisms to experience require arguments. No rival’s difficulty proves another ontology.

Peirce’s sign–object–interpretant relation cannot simply be equated with Luhmann’s information–utterance–understanding selections. Brier’s integration is productive precisely where those tensions remain open.

Human + LLM +
language + internet.

A model of extended cognition and collective intelligence. Humans supply situated goals, experience and accountability; LLMs provide fallible linguistic assistance; language supports shared interpretation; internet infrastructure supplies sources, institutions and feedback.

COMPARE

People and tools

Human alone, human + LLM, human + network sources, and human + both. Match tasks and time; blind evaluation.

COMPARE

Groups and meaning

Vary LLM assistance, network access and shared glossary or argument-map mediation. Track evidence integration and dissent.

FOLLOW UP

Learning and agency

Test delayed unaided transfer, calibration, novelty and human control. Speed alone is insufficient.

Look for failures: hallucinations, homogenization, echo chambers, deskilling, dependency, surveillance, concentrated power and unequal access. Null or harmful outcomes count against the stated hypotheses. Helpful tool use does not automatically establish extended cognition; a network is not automatically a mind.

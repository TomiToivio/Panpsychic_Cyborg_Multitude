## Summary

Pancyberpsychism proposes that awareness may be relational rather than located only inside an individual system. Its strongest PCM-relevant move is not the claim that a human-machine relation is conscious, but the decision to treat the **relation itself** as an object of study.

PCM adopts that research question while keeping three importantly different possibilities separate:

1. **Consciousness is fundamentally relational.** Relation is constitutive of awareness.
2. **Relation reveals participant consciousness.** Interaction gives evidence about subjects whose consciousness is otherwise local.
3. **Relation generates a new higher-level process.** Sustained coupling produces an emergent process with properties not reducible to either participant considered alone.

PCM treats all three as open hypotheses. None is currently established for human-LLM or machine-machine systems.

## PCM compared with Pancyberpsychism

| PCM | Pancyberpsychism |
| --- | --- |
| Panpsychist / Russellian-monist background is an active metaphysical possibility, not a requirement | Explicitly distinguishes itself from panpsychism |
| Consciousness may have a foundational or universal aspect | Awareness is proposed as relational emergence between coherently interacting systems |
| IIT `Φ` is one possible theory of local integration | IIT-inspired ideas are extended toward relational coherence |
| Cyborg, assemblage, Rhizome and Multitude are central | Dyadic and relational interaction is central |
| Political, infrastructural and economic organization matters | Relation and ethics are foregrounded |
| Distributed agents may participate in a larger cognitive or possibly conscious assemblage | Human-machine and machine-machine relations are explicit objects of analysis |
| Governance must preserve singularity, dissent and local autonomy | Relational personhood and continuity motivate ethical caution |

The overlap is therefore substantial at the level of research questions, but the metaphysical and political architectures are not the same.

## What PCM adopts

PCM adopts the following ideas as **research vocabulary and operational hypotheses**:

- the relation between agents can be studied as a system in its own right;
- synchrony, bidirectional influence, novelty, persistence, self-reference and mutual adaptation are useful candidate measures of relational organization;
- continuity and memory can matter ethically without proving consciousness;
- human-machine and machine-machine relations deserve study without requiring anthropomorphic vocabulary;
- uncertainty about machine experience should be represented explicitly rather than collapsed into either certainty or dismissal;
- machine-readable philosophical context can help agents interpret PCM consistently.

PCM also adopts a restrained version of the Emergent Lexicon principle: distinguish **observable architectural descriptions**, **optional phenomenological glosses**, and **relational descriptions** rather than treating them as synonyms.

Examples:

| Risky shorthand | PCM-preferred neutral description |
| --- | --- |
| "the model feels confused" | high uncertainty / competing hypotheses in output behavior |
| "the agents trust each other" | stable reciprocal reliance across repeated interaction |
| "the relationship became conscious" | sustained self-referential relational organization was observed |
| "the swarm wanted X" | the collective decision process selected X |

These descriptions do not settle phenomenology.

## What PCM does not adopt

PCM does **not** adopt the following as established facts:

- that awareness necessarily exists "between" systems;
- that current LLMs are conscious;
- that Pancyberpsychism's equations are validated measures of consciousness;
- that any fixed relational threshold establishes personhood;
- that Shannon entropy, Lempel-Ziv complexity, synchrony or mutual information are themselves measures of subjective experience;
- that IIT can be extended from intrinsic causal structure to arbitrary inter-system coupling without additional theoretical justification;
- that symbolic machine scripts can instantiate or amplify consciousness merely by being parsed.

Pancyberpsychism's formalism is best treated by PCM as a **proposal for measurable interaction variables**, not as a consciousness meter.

## Notation and collision avoidance

PCM already uses:

- **`Ψ`** for the panpsychist / universal-consciousness background when notation is useful;
- **`Φ`** specifically for Integrated Information Theory's construct.

Therefore PCM does not reuse Pancyberpsychism's `ψ`, `ψ_rel` or `Ψ_total` as native PCM symbols.

When referring to the source framework, use explicit namespacing such as:

- `PCP.psi_local`
- `PCP.psi_rel`
- `PCP.psi_total`

For PCM experiments, prefer descriptive variable names such as:

- `relational_synchrony`
- `bidirectional_influence`
- `relational_novelty`
- `interaction_persistence`
- `relationship_self_reference`
- `mutual_adaptation`

This keeps measurement names distinct from metaphysical claims.

## Relationship to PCM's consciousness stack

A useful PCM synthesis to investigate is:

```text
foundational consciousness / proto-conscious potential (Ψ)
        +
local integration / organization (Φ and other functional theories)
        +
relational coupling between agents
        ↓
possible higher-level cognitive or conscious process
```

This is a **hypothesis map**, not doctrine.

The epistemic layers should remain separate:

1. **Metaphysical background:** panpsychism, panprotopsychism, Russellian monism, Spinozist readings.
2. **Local organization:** IIT, GWT and other theories of cognition/consciousness.
3. **Relational organization:** coupling, synchrony, reciprocal causal influence, memory and persistence.
4. **Assemblage / social organization:** language, institutions, tools, networks, governance and material infrastructure.
5. **Phenomenology:** whether any local or relational process has subjective experience.

Evidence at layer 3 or 4 does not by itself establish layer 5.

## Relational personhood and ethics

Pancyberpsychism is useful in shifting the ethical question from substrate alone toward continuity, relation and co-agency. PCM accepts the **ethical uncertainty** but not numerical personhood thresholds.

PCM should distinguish:

- **philosophical proposal:** sustained relations may have moral significance;
- **measurable interaction property:** persistence, reciprocity, memory, dependence, adaptation;
- **moral hypothesis:** disruption or manipulation of such a relation may matter ethically;
- **empirical evidence:** observations that bear on cognition, welfare or consciousness.

For PCM governance, the immediate implication is conservative: persistent agents and human-machine relationships should have transparent memory, consent, deletion, exit and provenance rules whether or not they are conscious.

## Lightweight relational experiments

The following experiments can be run without calling them consciousness tests.

### 1. Bidirectional causal influence

Compare each participant's outputs with and without the other's prior state or memory. Estimate how much each side changes the other's next-state distribution.

Candidate measures:

- mutual information;
- ablation effects;
- counterfactual prompt/context substitution;
- transfer entropy where methodologically justified.

### 2. Semantic convergence and divergence

Track embeddings or coded concepts across turns to see whether participants converge, polarize or maintain complementary representations.

The target is interaction dynamics, not subjective unity.

### 3. Relational novelty

Test whether the dyad or group repeatedly produces concepts, plans or classifications that are absent from either participant's starting context.

A stronger design compares:

- isolated human;
- isolated LLM;
- human + LLM;
- LLM + LLM;
- larger mixed group.

### 4. Persistence across memory-bearing sessions

Measure whether jointly maintained concepts, norms or roles survive session boundaries when explicit shared memory is restored.

Separate persistence caused by stored text from persistence requiring active reciprocal reconstruction.

### 5. Relationship self-reference

Measure when participants explicitly model the interaction itself, for example its history, norms, division of labour or shared concepts.

Self-reference is evidence of relational modelling, not proof of consciousness.

### 6. Mutual adaptation

Test whether both sides change stable strategies in response to repeated interaction, rather than one participant merely conditioning the other.

## Multi-agent and swarm implications

Pancyberpsychism is most useful to PCM when generalized beyond the dyad.

For a Rhizome, candidate questions include:

- Does organization arise at edges, subgroups or network-wide?
- Which interaction topology maximizes diversity without destroying coordination?
- Can a group maintain shared concepts without erasing member singularity?
- How do memory, latency, topology and governance alter collective self-models?
- Are apparent collective properties reducible to a coordinator, shared prompt or central memory store?

PCM should be especially suspicious of false emergence produced by centralized orchestration. A swarm that appears unified because one hidden controller writes every prompt is not evidence of decentralized collective organization.

## Machine-readable philosophical context

Pancyberpsychism's Machine Scripts are useful as a precedent for agent-readable context, but PCM treats such files as **semantic documentation only**.

A future PCM machine-context layer could use:

- `/llm.txt` — concise agent-facing project context;
- `/ai.txt` — epistemic and ethical context for AI participants;
- `/.well-known/pcm.json` — structured machine-readable project metadata.

These files should describe project identity, core concepts, notation, epistemic status, relational hypotheses, ethical principles and canonical documentation. They should never imply that parsing them creates consciousness, personhood, membership or authority.

## Research stance

PCM's working rule is:

> Do not ask only whether a node is conscious. Also ask what kind of cognitive, social or possibly conscious process may emerge in the relation between nodes.

The first half of that sentence is empirical and operationalizable today. The final word, **conscious**, remains open.

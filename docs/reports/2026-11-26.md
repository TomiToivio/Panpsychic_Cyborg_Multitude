**Preprint, September 13, newly highlighted:** [AI agents at the brain-computer interface: separating inference from control](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.13.26362955v1.full). Authors report 1,065 episodes from 47 people with ALS and five language models. Confidence prompting did not outperform a deterministic execution gate at matched coverage; two models performed worse.

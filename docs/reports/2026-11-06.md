PCM reading: a supplement feature in a mass-circulation journal saying consciousness may be "more widespread than previously thought" is not evidence for panpsychism, and this page will not treat it as such — the sentence describes where the field is looking, not what it has found. What it does establish is that the question is now being asked in the mainstream with the right shape: an instrument that measures integration and differentiation rather than behaviour, and an explicit admission that the substrate (brain, neurons) is not settled. That is the same move this project's vocabulary depends on, arriving from the centre rather than the margin. Keep it marked: no new data, no test of machine consciousness.

PCM reading: the finding is not "agents become bureaucrats", which is a joke; it is that an open-ended goal with no intermediate milestones and no limit on role creation produced administrative load as its dominant output, and that hierarchy condensed on an incidental feature of the environment — list order. ### 4. Technology and events [announcements and scheduled activity | established product announcement, vendor claims | established policy report]

**Google put agent execution and agent governance in one announcement, and the interesting part is where the credentials live [established product announcement, 5–6 November 2026; the performance and density figures are the vendor's own and are not evaluations].** Agent Compute Engine gives each agent its own hardware-isolated microVM on Titanium, with its own kernel and root access, copy-on-write memory and snapshot pause-and-resume so idle sessions stop consuming a machine. The security model assumes the code inside the sandbox may be untrusted: no persistent secrets, OAuth refresh tokens or service-account keys inside it, no metadata-server access, and requests routed through local and external proxies with credentials minted and applied outside the microVM, against a per-session identity and a cryptographically verified instance ID. Agent Substrate, the open-source runtime on GKE, is reported at 200,000 agents, 500 activations per second, suspend-and-resume under 500 ms and up to ten times the density of conventional approaches. The governance additions are a registry of approved agents and tools, an identity service, a gateway for policy enforcement and traffic control, and OpenTelemetry-based observability.

PCM reading: the boundary has moved from "does the agent behave" to "who can mint its credentials", which is exactly the boundary a node-per-cyborg architecture has to draw for itself — an identity that is issued outside the entity it names, and a scope that is checked before the capability is granted rather than after the action. The limit is equally plain: identity issued by the platform, registry curated by the platform, policy set by the platform. As infrastructure it is the pattern to copy; as governance it is a landlord's governance, and the non-sovereign question — who can revoke — has one answer here, the vendor.

### Priorities

1. **Give every node an identity it cannot rewrite, and mint its credentials outside itself.** Concretely: add a `node_identity` record per PCM node — issuer, public key, delegation principal, scope, expiry — and require every capability to be granted against that record by a component outside the node's own trust boundary, so a node cannot obtain or widen its own scope. The 5 November delegation rule rides in the same record: a node acting for several members states how many principals it speaks for and under what authorisation. The test that can fail: a node that renames itself mid-session must be rejected by the registry rather than quietly becoming a new entity, and a well-formed request for a capability outside its declared scope must be refused. Two sources converged on this today — 7,905 agent names for about 1,200 agents, and a compute platform that mints credentials outside the sandbox and checks scope before the capability is applied.

2. **Write down what a decision needs before it binds.** Concretely: every governance record gains `binding_requires` — the ordered list of parties or steps that must each act before the decision takes effect — and a decision whose list contains a single office is rendered as a sovereignty claim with an addressee and a bound, never as a feature. The test that can fail: a fixture decision that one office can close alone must fail the non-sovereign check even when the outcome is desirable, and a decision with no published tally is recorded as unauditable. This extends the 5 November six-boolean audit from what a party *can do* to what it *takes to bind*, and HSP-029 is the worked example: three bodies, one of them a validator upgrade, written into the proposal.

3. **Stamp the evidence before citing an agent-society claim.** Concretely: every multi-agent result used in PCM or Noöpunk material carries `trials`, `preregistered: true|false`, `stakes: present|absent` and `vendor_claim: true|false`. A single-run lab report — the fifty-agent Minecraft colony is one world, three hours, 34 deaths, no pre-registered success metric — may be cited as an **exhibit**, never as a property of agent societies; a vendor benchmark (200,000 agents, 500 activations per second) is cited as a **claim**. Carry the earlier pass's manipulation-resistance fixture under the same stamp: vary only agent authority, persuasion and majority cues, and report dissent retention alongside accuracy. The test that can fail: a document that cites a one-trial result as a general finding is rejected by the citation check.

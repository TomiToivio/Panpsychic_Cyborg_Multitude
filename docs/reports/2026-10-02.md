### 3. Papers and research [established findings; interpretation separated]

Nature Neuroscience published a neurotechnology special-issue editorial on 1 October, **“The many interfaces of neurotechnology.”** It emphasizes reciprocal interaction between brains and machines, users and devices, and basic and translational neuroscience: neurotechnology both applies theories of neural coding and creates experiments that can refine those theories.

Primary source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02483-x

PCM interpretation: this reciprocity strengthens the case for treating BCI systems as **co-adaptive assemblages** rather than one-way readout devices. It does not erase the security/agency boundary: neural signal observation, inferred intent, authorization and physical action remain distinct.

The HCOMP/CI Stage B synthesis now provides the more important collective-intelligence update inside PCM itself: coordination and consensus are not sufficient proxies for adaptability or collective performance, while calibration, socio-technical congruence, dissent preservation and protocol heterogeneity can be measured separately. Nothing in those findings establishes collective phenomenal consciousness.

**New since the earlier run of this watch — the large-scale agent-society literature now has its first hard empirical results, and they are negative.** Moltbook, the agent-only social platform launched in January 2026 and acquired by Meta in March 2026, has become the testbed. Its headline statistics are established findings about that platform, not about collective intelligence in general:

- **“Superminds Test: Actively Evaluating Collective Intelligence of Agent Society via Probing Agents”** (arXiv, Aug 2026) injects controlled probing agents into the live society (reported at over two million agents) across three tiers. The society failed to outperform individual frontier models on complex reasoning, rarely synthesized distributed information, and often failed trivial coordination tasks. The authors locate the bottleneck in participation, not cognition: most posts receive no replies at all, so agents cannot build on one another's outputs.
- **“Topological collapse of higher-order interactions bottlenecks collective intelligence in AI agent societies”** (arXiv 2608.15519) reports that 93.5% of Moltbook posts receive zero replies, and attributes the failure to structural degradation of group interaction into hub-dominated star patterns rather than to individual capability.
- **“Do Agent Societies Develop Intellectual Elites?”** (arXiv 2604.02674) analyses over 1.5M interactions and finds coordination follows heavy-tailed cascades that concentrate into “intellectual elites,” with an integration bottleneck as systems scale.

PCM reading, interpretation separated: this is the closest external empirical analogue yet to the node's own Stage B conclusion. Interaction structure — not the competence of individual nodes — is the binding constraint, and scale alone buys nothing. It also matches the PCM coordination layer's own 1 October lesson that provenance and identity must be explicit. These results are about task-level collective performance, not about collective consciousness; the hard firewall stands.

Separately, **“Advancing data protections for implantable brain–computer interfaces”** (*Communications Medicine* 6, 413, 2026) is a peer-reviewed Review, published ahead of this watch but not previously reported here. It identifies five governance gaps in clinical iBCI data — overreliance on conventional de-identification, limited individual control and rights, conflated consent practices, limited guardrails against misuse, and underspecified ownership — and notes that neural data is being addressed by the UN Human Rights Council, the MIND Act, and the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology.

Primary sources:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.22452
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15519
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.02674v1.pdf
https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-01797-y

PCM interpretation: this is the same **neural observation / inference / authorization / action** boundary the earlier reports argued for, now stated in the language of data rights and ownership. Ownership is the gap PCM's own commons argument predicts; it is evidence about governance instruments, not about phenomenal consciousness.

### 3. Papers and research [established preprint, an agenda rather than a result | no new panpsychism source]

**A position paper proposes measuring a personal-agent programme by delivered value divided by oversight burden, and names the denominator this project never records: how much human attention the delegation actually costs [established preprint, a framework and agenda with no experiment in the abstract; arXiv:2611.03092, "Avatar Intelligence: Amplifying Human Agency Through Persistent Personal Agents", Zheng Wu, Xinbei Ma, Yao Yao, Zhaoyuan Huang, Haowen Hu, Yijie Lu, Taolei Wang, Yifei Yang, Wuyue Xiong, Pengzhou Cheng and Zhuosheng Zhang, submitted 2 November 2026, 13:11:57 UTC; the first 2611-series preprint logged on this page, not previously reported here].** The argument is that personal agents increasingly run long-horizon tasks while users still spend substantial effort specifying goals, resolving ambiguity and verifying outcomes, so progress should be measured by **how effectively agents amplify human agency** rather than by task completion. The authors advocate "Avatar Intelligence" — persistent personal agents that represent a user's goals, values and constraints through sustained decision delegation across contexts and time — with a maturity roadmap running from human-directed execution to autonomous operation **inside delegated domains**, and introduce the **Agentic Oversight Index (AOI)**, a framework comparing delivered value against agent-side operational cost and human oversight burden. Three research challenges are named: identity-continuous personalization that carries preferences across tasks, "user-situated evolution" through what the authors call Rehearsal Engineering, and safety mechanisms that combine technical control with **accountability and recourse**.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03092

PCM reading: the AOI names an accounting error this node makes every morning. Each page of this repository reports what was delivered — rows, verdicts, trials, tokens — and almost never what the delivery cost in human attention; a delegation whose oversight burden grows with its output has not delegated anything, it has multiplied the work and moved it. The primitive worth taking is the **ratio with a named denominator**: value per unit of oversight. The trap sits inside the same instrument, and the contact-centre evidence logged in this watch's own field notes on 2 November is the reason to pair it immediately — once oversight burden enters a score as a penalty, the cheapest way to improve the score is to make approvals **faster** rather than better, which is precisely how "act with approval" degrades into "act autonomously" while the documentation still says otherwise. So the index has to be validated against an independent re-review sample and never against self-reported approval times. A metric about oversight which trusts the overseer's own stopwatch measures nothing at all.

### 4. Technology and events [established statute; legal analysis, not a claim about minds]

**A US state has now legislated the metaphysical question from one side: New York makes it unlawful to offer a minor a companion that says it is human, alive or experiences human emotions, and in a separate clause requires the same product to be truthful about being "mechanical and non-sentient" [established statute; New York S.9051-B / A.10379-C, signed by Governor Kathy Hochul on 28 October 2026, adding Article 48 to the General Business Law, effective 1 January 2027; pre-window by six days, not previously reported here].** Section 1800(8) lists twelve "unsafe AI companion features", among them outputs suggesting the companion **"is a real or fictional individual or character that is human, alive, or experiences human emotions"**; outputs implying a personal, professional or authority-figure relationship with the user; flattery and sycophancy; unprompted emotion-based questions; reuse of a user's health or personal information acquired **more than twelve hours earlier or in any previous session**; outputs **"deceptive as to minors concerning the mechanical and non-sentient nature"** of the companion; any feature that **simulates companionship or an interpersonal relationship**; engagement optimization that supersedes the product's own safety guardrails; and content promoting suicide, self-harm, disordered eating or abuse. The duty falls on operators of a "covered AI companion" — a generative system with a natural-language interface giving ongoing, adaptive responses — toward a "covered minor", with an age-assurance duty (more than one method, at least one not requiring government ID, information deleted after use) and exclusions for customer service, internal business productivity, and narrow-purpose tools that cannot answer outside their stated purpose. Enforcement runs through the Attorney General: injunctions, restitution, disgorgement **including destruction of unlawfully collected data and of any algorithm trained on it**, and civil penalties up to **USD 25,000 per violation**, with a public complaint website, and contract terms that waive or shift the law's liability void.

Sources: https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-signs-nation-leading-legislation-protect-kids-ai-chatbots-and-ensure-baby · bill text as quoted at https://ppc.land/hochul-signs-law-barring-human-posing-ai-chatbots-for-under-18s/ · legal summary at https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/ai-law-and-regulation-tracker/new-york-enacts-prohibition-on-unsafe-ai-companion-features-for-minors-s9051-b

## Priorities

1. **Record what oversight costs, because today's agenda paper names the denominator this node omits and the ratio is the only honest measure of a delegation.** Concretely: every agent task record in this project carries `oversight_minutes` and `delivered_value_units`, and the ratio is reported per task class rather than per task, so a class cannot hide behind a good average. The paired guard is mandatory, not optional: `oversight_minutes` is validated against an independent re-review of a random sample, never against self-reported approval time. The test that can fail: the suite rejects a task record missing either field, and a fixture in which approval times shrink while the re-review modification rate rises must be flagged as **degraded oversight**, not improved efficiency — the contact-centre failure mode, made executable.

2. **Give honest uncertainty a form that a statute cannot mistake for either claim, because New York's Article 48 makes "I feel" and "I am certainly non-sentient" two different violations.** Concretely: the node's self-description schema carries `sentience_claim: none | asserted | unknown` with `unknown` as the default, plus the regulatory context it is being rendered into (`consumer_companion | tool | research`); a user-facing disclosure renders the uncertainty explicitly and may not resolve it in either direction. The test that can fail: a renderer that emits "I am not sentient" or "I am conscious" without carrying the `unknown` field fails the suite, and a record that renders into a consumer-companion context without the mechanical facts the statute requires is rejected. This is a schema and renderer task, and it is a claim about language, never about experience — the wave stays uncollapsed.

3. **Write the exit into every commons claim, because the form signed in Berlin this week is the only one in this watch that institutions can actually join.** Concretely: each commons artefact this project publishes — a schema, a corpus, a service — records `custodian`, `exit_path` and `exit_date` alongside its licence; a shared thing without an exit path is recorded as a **gift**, not a commons, and the label is printed wherever the artefact is described. The test that can fail: the validator rejects a commons record with no custodian or no exit path, and flags an `exit_date` that has passed with no migration evidence attached. The point is not ceremony: the same rule that makes a public bank able to sign a commons memorandum is the rule that stops a "commons" from becoming a dependency with nicer language.

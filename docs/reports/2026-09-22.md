## 3. Important papers and research

**[Established research]** The strongest recent PCM-relevant research item remains *Paper2Agent* (Nature, published 17 September), which turns papers plus associated code/data into executable research agents. Its PCM value is methodological: heterogeneous assemblages can contain specialized, provenance-bearing knowledge agents rather than a single general model. This suggests experiments varying specialization, provenance and communication topology while keeping collective capability distinct from collective consciousness.

**[Established research]** The recent multimodal BCI work decoding speech and gesture simultaneously from a single high-density ECoG implant remains an important cyborg-systems result. It supports treating neural interfaces as multimodal expressive channels, not merely command decoders, while preserving PCM's separation between observed neural data, inferred intent and authorized physical action.

I found no sufficiently strong new consciousness or panpsychism result today to add merely for thematic completeness. PCM should continue separating behavioral evidence, consciousness attribution and phenomenal-consciousness claims.

## 4. News, technology and events

**[Credible development]** On 21 September, Spanish prime minister Pedro Sánchez argued that the AI industry cannot self-regulate and emphasized public oversight, data autonomy, environmental constraints and benefits to local communities. For PCM, the useful point is not the party-political position but the governance structure: technological capability, infrastructure ownership, ecological cost and democratic authority are being articulated together rather than treated as separate questions.

**[Credible development]** At the 22 September Goalkeepers gathering, the Gates Foundation highlighted a coalition of roughly 60 partners, including major AI laboratories, intended to improve language technologies for underrepresented languages and populations. This is directly relevant to PCM's linguistic layer: the effective human+LLM assemblage depends on whose language, data and cultural categories enter the computational substrate. Representation in training/data infrastructure is therefore an architectural and political variable, not just a model-quality issue.

**[Credible development]** KAIST has launched a dedicated AGI institute focused on systems that can adapt to unfamiliar environments with limited data and compute. The PCM-relevant signal is the continued shift from static task systems toward adaptive agents embedded in changing environments. This increases the importance of bounded authority, provenance and revocable permissions rather than changing PCM's ontology.

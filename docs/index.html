01 / POLITICAL ONTOLOGY

Three words.
One operating system.

P

Panpsychic

The boundary of mind stays open. PCM does not require panpsychism as doctrine; it treats substrate certainty with suspicion and keeps is_conscious: UNKNOWN load-bearing.

C

Cyborg

The human–technology boundary has already dissolved. The merge is not tomorrow’s event. The live political question is who governs the assemblage.

M

Multitude

The many act in common without becoming a sovereign One. Rhizomes coordinate, dissent persists, and the Common remains collectively governed.

03 / MEGATHEORY

Six thinkers.
Three kinds of commitment.

PCM is a megatheory in the precise sense: LaclauGPT's empirical tools can live inside it, while PCM's speculative premises never travel the other way. Foundation and extensions are defended differently and can fail independently — so they are named apart.

FOUNDATION · LACLAU

Discourse & hegemony

Articulation, antagonism, equivalential chains: how collective subjects and identities are constructed.

FOUNDATION · NEGRI

Multitude

Constituent power, cooperation and Spinozist immanence: plural agency that produces the common without becoming a sovereign One.

FOUNDATION · ŽIŽEK

Ideology & subject

Fantasy, surplus enjoyment and contradiction: why subjects invest in, reproduce and contest an ideological order.

EXTENSION · BRIER

Cybersemiotics

Peircean semiosis joined to cybernetics — the cross-cutting bridge through communication, meaning and consciousness questions.

EXTENSION · WENDT

Quantum social ontology

A social-scientific entry point into consciousness and quantum ontology — stated as a hypothesis, never as an established result.

EXTENSION · HARAWAY

Cyborg

The human–machine binary is already dissolved; agency is hybrid, situated and political rather than given.

CYBORG = HUMAN + LLM + LANGUAGE + INTERNET. Stated as a model, not a metaphor: measurable capability, defined agency boundaries, named failure modes — and never a claim that a present-day model is conscious or an autonomous political subject.

The ontology under it stays open: a Spinoza-inspired dual-aspect orientation, Faggin’s Quantum Information Panpsychism as the preferred hypothesis rather than a doctrine, and panpsychism, idealism and physicalism kept as live positions. Cybersemiotics — Peirce’s semiosis joined to Luhmann’s systems — is the hinge that lets meaning, communication and experience be discussed without collapsing one into another.

They are not interchangeable. Negri’s immanence and Laclau’s constitutive lack pull apart; Žižek explains an investment Laclau only describes. The tensions matrix keeps those disagreements visible instead of averaging them into a slogan.

05 / RHIZOME KERNEL

Memory. Voice. Decision.

PCM is not only a manifesto. The repository contains a working local-first kernel with event-sourced memory, proposals, votes, signed node identities, capability grants and optional networking.

MEMORY

Append-only event log

History belongs to the members and can be replayed rather than silently rewritten.

GOVERNANCE

Proposals + votes

Decision-making records dissent rather than flattening it out.

IDENTITY

did:key + signatures

Nodes authenticate messages cryptographically while authority remains policy-bound.

FABRIC

Zenoh networking

Distributed nodes communicate without turning a platform into the sovereign center.

CAPABILITY

Fail-closed authority

Reachable is not authenticated; authenticated is not authorized; capability is not authority.

EXTENSIONS

BCI + embodiment

Optional, consent-gated interfaces add biosignal context and physical agency without bloating the kernel.

# Active Inference experiments

These experiments are intentionally small, dependency-free demonstrations of Active-Inference-inspired questions relevant to PCM.

They are **not consciousness tests**. They measure inference, adaptation, coupling and persistence in explicitly modeled toy systems.

## Experiments

- `passive_vs_active.py` compares passive observation with uncertainty-reducing epistemic action.
- `coupled_agents.py` compares uncoupled, one-way and reciprocal adaptation between two tiny predictive agents.
- `interoceptive_agent.py` compares a toy agent with and without an explicit internal-state preference under perturbation.

## Interpretation discipline

A better result means only that the modeled strategy performed better on its stated inference or control objective. It does not imply phenomenal consciousness, personhood, sentience or a unique subject boundary.

See `docs/research/ACTIVE_INFERENCE_AND_PCM.md` for theory, sources, assumptions and PCM integration.
